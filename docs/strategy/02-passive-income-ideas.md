# 15 tourism digital-product ideas, ranked

Ranked from easiest money to highest long-term potential. Items 1-5 are close to passive but have low
ceilings; items 11-15 are SaaS and need ongoing support. Market figures come from vendor and analyst
blogs and are secondhand (tour operator software ~$0.8-0.9B in 2026, ~10-12% CAGR; Airbnb 9M+ listings,
5M+ hosts).

## Tier 1: quick cash (days to 2 weeks)
1. **Destination-specific STR welcome-book kits** (Crete, Budapest, Balaton; EN/DE/HU/GR). Canva + pre-written local content. 3-5 days. Etsy/Payhip.
2. **AI prompt and message pack for hosts/operators.** Listing copy, guest messages, review replies. ~1 week. Gumroad. Easily copied; use as lead magnet.
3. **Notion/Sheets STR operations system.** Turnovers, cleaners, inventory, revenue. 1-2 weeks. Notion Marketplace, Gumroad.
4. **Tour operator pricing and margin calculator (Sheets).** Cost per head, break-even group size, OTA commission scenarios. ~1 week. Gumroad/Etsy; B2B buyers pay $29-49.
5. **Niche digital travel guides.** 2-3 weeks. Own site, KDP. Needs traffic; do last in tier 1.

## Tier 2: productized tools (3-6 weeks)
6. **Tour operator website template** (Framer/Webflow). 3-4 weeks.
7. **Mini-course: AI automation for small tour/STR businesses.** 3-4 weeks.
8. **WordPress plugin: tour schema and AI-search visibility.** 4-5 weeks. Freemium on WordPress.org + Freemius Pro.
9. **QR-code mobile guidebook micro-SaaS** (natural upsell from #1). 4-6 weeks. Competes with Touchstay/Hostfully; differentiate on price, multilingual AI content, regional focus.
10. **AI review-reply / guest-message Chrome extension.** 3-4 weeks. Breaks if OTA layouts change.

## Tier 3: highest long-term potential (6-12+ weeks)
11. **Seasonality and events demand calendar** (Budapest, Crete...). Feed/calendar/API. **Tourism Pulse already covers most of this.**
12. **Regional tourism intelligence subscription.** Monthly reports/dashboard for Greece/Hungary. **This is Tourism Pulse's monetization path.** Data sourcing is the bottleneck.
13. **Tour operator OTA payout and reconciliation tool** (Viator, GetYourGuide, direct). 8-10 weeks.
14. **Multilingual AI guest concierge (WhatsApp)** with upsell commissions. 8-12 weeks.
15. **White-label AI itinerary and upsell widget** for tour operators (reported ~13% conversion lift for AI itineraries). 10-12 weeks. Highest ceiling.

## Suggested funnel
Run #1 -> #9 as one funnel (welcome-book buyers become guidebook SaaS leads). Add #4 as the tour-operator wedge;
its buyers seed the beta list for #15. Tourism Pulse feeds #11/#12 and gives #1 better local content than generic competitors.
